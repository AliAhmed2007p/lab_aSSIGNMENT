Sign in once, use both applications

The platform below is the central identity provider. It holds the user directory, the organizations, the security policies and the tokens. The two applications ask it who you are - they never see or store your password.

  1. Pick an application; it sends you to the central sign-in page.
  2. Sign in with your registration number or staff username (add a second factor if your organization requires it).
  3. Open the second application - you are already signed in, no credentials asked again.
